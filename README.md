## 🌟 Overview

**HENSHE** is a modern, visual-first, high-impact web platform engineered for a forward-thinking Indian electric vehicle mobility group. Designed to be **super catchy, trending, impressive, and visual**, explaining core advantages with real EV scooty photography and concise value propositions.

The platform directly serves two major stakeholders:
1. **🛵 For Gig Delivery Riders (Zomato, Swiggy, Blinkit, Zepto, Porter):**
   - Rent commercial electric scooters starting from ₹180/day.
   - Zero petrol cost — save ₹4,500+ every month.
   - Unlimited 2-minute battery swaps at Gurugram & Noida Hubs.
   - Instant KYC (Aadhaar & Driving Licence) with same-day pickup.
   - 100% covered hub maintenance and zero out-of-pocket repair bills.

2. **📈 For Smart Fleet Investors (Retail & HNI Investors):**
   - Purchase physical electric scooters registered in your name (RC ownership).
   - Earn predictable contractual monthly bank payouts (18% – 24% target yields).
   - Benefit from up to 40% accelerated tax depreciation.
   - 100% hands-off management handled by HENSHE's central hubs.
